Surah 111: Al-Lahab (The Flame)

#Transliteration (Tanzil.net) & Translation (Pickthall)
1Tabbat yada abee lahabin watabba
The power of Abu Lahab will perish, and he will perish.
2Ma aghna AAanhu maluhu wama kasaba
His wealth and gains will not exempt him.
3Sayasla naran thata lahabin
He will be plunged in flaming Fire,
4Waimraatuhu hammalata alhatabi
And his wife, the wood-carrier,
5Fee jeediha hablun min masadin
Will have upon her neck a halter of palm-fibre.
