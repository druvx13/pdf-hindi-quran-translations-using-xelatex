Surah 102: At-Takathur (The Abundance of Wealth)

#Transliteration (Tanzil.net) & Translation (Pickthall)
1Alhakumu alttakathuru
Rivalry in worldly increase distracteth you
2Hatta zurtumu almaqabira
Until ye come to the graves.
3Kalla sawfa taAAlamoona
Nay, but ye will come to know!
4Thumma kalla sawfa taAAlamoona
Nay, but ye will come to know!
5Kalla law taAAlamoona AAilma alyaqeeni
Nay, would that ye knew (now) with a sure knowledge!
6Latarawunna aljaheema
For ye will behold hell-fire.
7Thumma latarawunnaha AAayna alyaqeeni
Aye, ye will behold it with sure vision.
8Thumma latusalunna yawmaithin AAani alnnaAAeemi
Then, on that day, ye will be asked concerning pleasure.
