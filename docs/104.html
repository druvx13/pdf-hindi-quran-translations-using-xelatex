Surah 104: Al-Humazah (The Slanderer)

#Transliteration (Tanzil.net) & Translation (Pickthall)
1Waylun likulli humazatin lumazatin
Woe unto every slandering traducer,
2Allathee jamaAAa malan waAAaddadahu
Who hath gathered wealth (of this world) and arranged it.
3Yahsabu anna malahu akhladahu
He thinketh that his wealth will render him immortal.
4Kalla layunbathanna fee alhutamati
Nay, but verily he will be flung to the Consuming One.
5Wama adraka ma alhutamatu
Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Consuming One is!
6Naru Allahi almooqadatu
(It is) the fire of Allah, kindled,
7Allatee tattaliAAu AAala alafidati
Which leapeth up over the hearts (of men).
8lnnaha AAalayhim musadatun
Lo! it is closed in on them
9Fee AAamadin mumaddadatin
In outstretched columns.
