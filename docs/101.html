Surah 101: Al-Qari'ah (The Calamity)

#Transliteration (Tanzil.net) & Translation (Pickthall)
1AlqariAAatu
The Calamity!
2Ma alqariAAatu
What is the Calamity?
3Wama adraka ma alqariAAatu
Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Calamity is!
4Yawma yakoonu alnnasu kaalfarashi almabthoothi
A day wherein mankind will be as thickly-scattered moths
5Watakoonu aljibalu kaalAAihni almanfooshi
And the mountains will become as carded wool.
6Faamma man thaqulat mawazeenuhu
Then, as for him whose scales are heavy (with good works),
7Fahuwa fee AAeeshatin radiyatin
He will live a pleasant life.
8Waamma man khaffat mawazeenuhu
But as for him whose scales are light,
9Faommuhu hawiyatun
A bereft and Hungry One will be his mother,
10Wama adraka ma hiyah
Ah, what will convey unto thee what she is! -
11Narun hamiyatun
Raging Fire.
